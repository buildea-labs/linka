The hero of the speed test: a thin circular progress ring wrapping one giant tabular-nums number that eases up toward the measured value. During the initial (invisible-to-copy) connecting phase the ring hides and the number shrinks to a muted "Preparando" label.

```jsx
<MetricRing progress={0.62} value="184,3" unit="Mbps" />
<MetricRing connecting value="Preparando" />
```
